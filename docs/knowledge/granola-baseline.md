# Granola baseline — the proven mechanics

What we're legally copying at pixel level. Per Pincus: don't question it, don't try to
improve it, don't test it — it's proven, and it's what users come back for.

## Mechanics to copy

- **Live transcript.** Real-time transcription running alongside the conversation
  (the thing Gary Tan explicitly likes about Granola).
- **Auto notes.** Structured notes generated from the transcript without user effort.
- **Shareable output.** Notes/briefs formatted to send to other people (Tan: his
  voice-memo + own-prompts flow wins today on being "way better and more sharable" —
  the bar for our output).

## What we're NOT copying

- The meeting-bot model (joining calls as a participant). Our better: no bot to invite —
  ambient, just on.
- The summary format. Our new/better: a **judgment brief** — commitments, deadlines,
  decisions, each with calibrated confidence — not a summary.

## Why Granola is the proven pick

Pincus: "probably the most successful AI notetaker product." Deconstruct it, copy everything
we're not innovating on, spend zero innovation budget here. All testing budget goes to the
new (always-listening + judgment).
